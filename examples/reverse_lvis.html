LVIS-92i: click only vs. click + CMAS (text → click), SAM3

Steering vectors from extra text guidance added to click-only inference: no class name at test time. Per class, five annotated support images: activations with an optimised class-text embedding + click minus the same click alone with SAM3's generic visual prompt; site and α per fold by leave-one-support-out: encoder.0.cross_attn in all three folds, α = 0.79 / 0.80 / 0.78. At test, one random positive click per (image, class) pair, identical in all click arms. Dataset result (paper Tables S11, S13): click 0.679 → 0.697 (+0.017 Dice, 3,436 episodes better / 3,204 worse, p = 4×10−12 over 6,900 episodes; among pairs changing by more than 0.05: 262 better / 140 worse); text + click 0.773. Mean of the shown pairs (not representative): click 0.60 → 0.78, text + click 0.75. Masks re-predicted with the paper's test code unchanged (same vectors and clicks); every mask reproduces the stored test record exactly.
